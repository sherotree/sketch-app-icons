## Longer blurb (PR body / homepage)

App Icons Finder searches the Apple App Store and inserts an official app icon as a PNG into your Sketch document.

Open **Plugins › App Icons Finder › Search App Icons**, type an app name, choose a store region, and pick a result. Insert PNG places a 128×128 image on the selected frame or group, or on the current page.

Search text and the region you pick are sent to Apple. Uwarp does not receive them. Icon files download from Apple only when you insert one.

App icons belong to their publishers. Use this for design reference, and get permission before you publish an icon.
